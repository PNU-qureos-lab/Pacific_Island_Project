Shallow-water candidate validation

Scene: 20240925_S2A · Grid: 20 m · ROIs: 30 windows of 7 × 7 pixels.

Depth status: no measured bathymetry was available. These are satellite-identified shallow-water candidates, selected from mapped water 80–500 m from non-water and brighter than the median nearshore water. This checks correction behaviour over likely shallow reef water; it does not prove water depth.

1. Shallow-water ROI selection

The cyan boundary is the candidate area. Yellow numbered points are the fixed ROIs used in every later calculation. Land, cloud and non-water SCL classes are excluded, and every 7 × 7 window must contain valid water.

How to read the upper-left graph: the ROIs must occur around the near-reef water rather than in the former offshore validation area.

2. Original glint-related signal

For every ROI, the mean of the smaller raw B11 or B12 value is recorded as a surface-reflection indicator. It is supporting glint evidence, not water depth and not pure glint.

How to read the lower-left graph: if the amount removed rises with this independent SWIR signal, the correction is responding to a surface-related pattern. Cycle 0: r=0.439, p=0.015243. Cycle 1: r=0.528, p=0.002688.

3. Original, Cycle 0 and Cycle 1 spectra

The upper-right graph uses the same 30 ROIs at all three stages. Thin lines are individual ROIs; thick lines are the 30-ROI medians.

How to read it: Original → Cycle 0 is the first removal. Cycle 0 → Cycle 1 is the accepted additional removal. Curves should remain smooth and should not collapse below zero.

5. Conclusion

The correction was applied and checked over independently selected shallow-water candidate ROIs. The report can state whether the spectra remain non-negative and whether the spatial shallow-bottom pattern is preserved. It cannot yet state that glint was perfectly removed or that depths are accurate, because measured depth or field Rrs was not available.
