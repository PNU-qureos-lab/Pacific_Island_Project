10. Final products

Rrs and rrs checks

Important: Rrs_approx = final L2A BOA reflectance / π. B01, B02, B03 and B04 provide the four nominal inversion wavelengths 443, 492, 560 and 665 nm. B01 is aligned to the 20 m grid but retains native 60 m spatial information. Below-surface rrs_approx = Rrs_approx / (0.52 + 1.7 Rrs_approx). These conversions passed numeric checks, but they are not field-validated water-leaving reflectance.
