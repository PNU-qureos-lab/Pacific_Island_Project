02 · Methods from the literature

What was applied—and what each result means

Modified Hedley · primary

Robust visible–NIR slope; subtract only positive NIR excess above the P1 deep-water baseline.

ρ′λ=ρλ−bλ·max(ρNIR−P1,0)

Lyzenga · sensitivity

Same covariance slope family, referenced to the mean NIR of the training water.

ρ′λ=ρλ−bλ(ρNIR−mean)

Joyce · sensitivity

Uses the modal training-water NIR as the assumed baseline.

ρ′λ=ρλ−bλ(ρNIR−mode)

Goodman · adapted

Original AVIRIS constants with Planet 666/866 nm substitutes. Tested, but not sensor-calibrated.

ρ′λ=ρλ−ρ866+A+B(ρ666−ρ866)

ACOLITE-NIR · experimental

ACOLITE residual-glint arithmetic adapted to Planet 866 nm NIR after importing Planet SR. This is not standard SWIR ACOLITE.

What “fixed regression-training water” means
It is a set of open-water pixels selected before fitting and then locked for a fair comparison. It is not ice, not ground truth, and not one small rectangle. Hedley, Lyzenga and Joyce use these pixels to estimate visible–NIR relationships; Goodman and ACOLITE-NIR do not fit their transfer rule from them.

06 · Sentinel‑2 B12 benchmark

B12 remains a reference—not a Planet input

Correct interpretation

Sentinel‑2 B12 provides a SWIR surface-reference channel that Planet does not measure. We compare method behaviour, spectral preservation and failure indicators; we do not describe Planet NIR as a substitute B12 observation.

No direct sensor ranking

The Planet and Sentinel‑2 acquisitions have different dates, resolutions and viewing conditions. This is a methodological benchmark, not pixel-matched validation.

08 · Final decision

Retain one primary Planet product and two sensitivity products

Primary: modified Hedley P1

Best overall balance: strong NIR decorrelation, virtually no negative pixels, conservative positive-excess correction, and slightly better CLR agreement than Original Planet SR.

Sensitivity: Lyzenga and Joyce

Useful for showing how the assumed glint-free NIR baseline changes the answer. They should not be counted as fully independent physical methods.

Reject for benthic production

Goodman and ACOLITE-NIR lie closer to CLR inside the fixed ROIs, but generate widespread negative pixels across the water mask. The local model match does not override this physical failure.

Scientific status: the selected Hedley GeoTIFFs are suitable as candidate preprocessing products for comparative benthic modelling. They are not validated water-leaving reflectance. Final ecological use still requires reef-control checks and preferably matched field spectra or a near-date SWIR-capable observation.
