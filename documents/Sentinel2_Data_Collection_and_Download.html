Collection method

  1. Search Sentinel‑2 catalogue products by the actual AOI footprint, acquisition window and product type.
  2. Retain every scene during discovery; do not reject a window because all available scenes are cloudy.
  3. Rank products separately for L1C and L2A. Choose the closest acquisition at or below 20% catalogue cloud. If none qualifies, retain the lowest-cloud fallback.
  4. Review the manifest before download. L1C is suitable for the planned ACOLITE atmospheric-correction workflow; L2A is retained for comparison and rapid screening.
  5. Authenticate using CDSE_USER and CDSE_PASS, then download with resume, archive validation and SAFE extraction.

Important: CDSE cloud percentage describes the full Sentinel‑2 product footprint, not only shallow water inside the study AOI. Visual inspection and water-specific screening remain necessary.
